Privacy Policy – Ohmiva

Updated: 5 September 2026

Summary

Ohmiva is designed as an offline app. The app requires no registration, user account or own server.

Data processed

Calculations and searches are processed locally on the device and are not transmitted to the provider or third parties.

  • favorite tools,
  • display/theme and language settings,
  • local acknowledgement of the current safety notices,
  • optional parts-box entries containing component name, value/type, quantity and a user note.

These data remain in the app's local storage.

Parts box

The parts box is a purely local inventory. Entries can be searched, changed or deleted. For manual export, the inventory can be copied as CSV text to the clipboard. The app does not upload this data to a server.

Network

The app does not request Android INTERNET permission. Calculators, reference content, favorites and the parts box work without a network connection.

Permissions

The app requires no sensitive Android runtime permissions for its intended core functions. It does not request camera permission.

Backup

Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Deletion

Local app data can be removed through Android system settings using “Clear app data”. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data.

Google Play

Google Play may process technically necessary data outside the app as part of operating the store. This processing is outside Ohmiva's local core functions. Google's privacy information applies to that processing.
